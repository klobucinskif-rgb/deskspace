Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do clients need an account?

No. A client opens the review link you send, watches the file, comments and approves. Clients don't take a seat on your plan.

What can a client do on the review link?

Comment on a file or on the exact second of a video, ask for changes, and approve the version. Once it's approved, the final files are available on that same link.

What happens to older versions?

They stay on the file. v1, v2 and v3 keep their own comments, and the team can see which version was approved and when.

Which files can I upload?

Videos, photos and documents. Each file keeps its version history inside the client's project.

How do workspaces and team members work?

Free, Creator and Pro come with a personal space. Team has up to 2 workspaces and 5 team members, Agency up to 5 workspaces and 10 team members. Your personal space counts as one of the workspaces.
