Do clients need an account?

No. A client opens the review link you send, watches the file, comments and approves. Clients don't take a seat on your plan.

What can a client do on the review link?

Comment on a file or on the exact second of a video, ask for changes, and approve the version. Once it is approved, the final files are on that same link.

What happens to older versions?

They stay on the file. v1, v2 and v3 keep their own comments, and the team can see which version was approved and when.

How do I add people to my team?

Show them the QR code, give them the join code, send the invite link, or invite them by email. Each person gets a role: Admin, Editor or Viewer.

How do teams and seats work?

Free, Creator and Pro come with a personal space. Team has up to 2 teams and 5 people, Agency up to 5 teams and 10 people. Your personal space counts as one of the teams.
